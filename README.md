
Do a retailer’s stock levels and discounts follow what customers actually want? Using engagement signals (loves, reviews, ratings) for 10,000 skincare products, this project segments products by demand and satisfaction and tests whether inventory and promotions line up. Key finding: the top 10% of products capture 57% of all customer loves, yet the most-loved products are no less likely to be low or out of stock. The dataset is synthetic, so findings illustrate the method rather than real market behavior.
